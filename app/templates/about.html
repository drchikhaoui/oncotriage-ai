OncoTriage AI is an open-source clinical decision support prototype for oncology symptom triage. It helps patients describe symptoms, applies COSTaRS v2020 evidence-based triage logic, and generates a triage recommendation (Emergency / Urgent / Routine / Self-Care).

Clinical Foundation

  • COSTaRS v2020 — evidence-based oncology triage rules
  • NCCN Clinical Practice Guidelines
  • CTCAE v5.0 adverse event grading
  • ASCO irAE Management Guidelines 2021
  • MASCC/ISOO/ESMO toxicity guidelines

AI Disclosure

The photo triage feature uses a fallback chain of AI vision providers (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, HuggingFace) for CTCAE visual grading. AI outputs are assistive only — all findings require clinical verification.

Privacy

No images are stored. Photos are processed in memory only and discarded immediately after analysis. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected. All triage sessions are stored anonymously.

Languages

English, French, and Arabic — with Tunisia-aware emergency numbers.
